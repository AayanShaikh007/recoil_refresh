An independent compatibility build based on SimpleCoil. This is a development build; compatibility with your phone and blaster still needs testing.

SimpleCoil is provided free with the hope that it will be useful. SimpleCoil comes with no warranty and may damage your hardware.

Use with RECOIL laser tag blasters. Your phone connects to its blaster over Bluetooth. Local play does not require Wi-Fi or a hub. Some features of the original Recoil app are not available here.

Tap the power button to toggle recoil. Tap the thumb/rear/talk button to switch firing modes.

Players that are on the same team cannot shoot each other - no friendly fire.

For network play, connect every phone to the same Wi-Fi and run Dedicated Server on one phone. The other phones join that server. If Wi-Fi has no internet, choose to stay connected. The original hub may provide Wi-Fi, but its built-in game server is not used. Hub Wi-Fi compatibility still needs testing. All players should use the same Refresh build.

Permissions

  • Bluetooth - to find and connect to your blaster.
  • Location - to show your position on the in-game map. Older versions of Android (before Android 12) also require it to search for Bluetooth devices. Your location is only shared with the game server and the other players in your game.
  • Camera - to scan the QR code on your blaster. Nothing is recorded or saved.
  • Nearby devices / local network and Wi-Fi - to find and talk to the other players and the game server on the same Wi-Fi network.
  • Notifications - optional; lets Android show the ongoing connection notification in your notification drawer. Denying it does not prevent blaster pairing.
  • Vibration - to buzz your phone when you get hit.
